How I Work

Three habits I bring to a support desk.

Write it down properly

Every ticket gets the issue, what I tried, and how it ended. An environment only one person understands is not supported, it is hostage.

Ask for a description, not a conclusion

“It is broken” tells you nothing. “What is on the screen right now, word for word” tells you a great deal. Most of troubleshooting is the first question.

Watch the pattern, not just the ticket

A queue that only closes tickets stays exactly as busy as it was. The one that notices what keeps returning gets quieter.

Community AI Initiative

IQRA Digital Skills & AI Readiness Initiative

Helping women, students, and families build digital confidence, safe technology habits, and beginner-friendly AI skills.

About the Initiative

Through my volunteer work with IQRA Arabic Academy I supported learners and teachers across software, hardware, devices, platforms, access, and training. IQRA began as a local learning effort and grew into an online community serving women and learners in different places.

The Problem

Many community learners have phones, apps, and online tools, but not the confidence to use technology for learning, safety, communication, or opportunity. As AI becomes more common, that gap widens unless people get practical, beginner-friendly guidance.

The Next Step

Expanding this into digital skills and AI readiness workshops: safe AI use, basic cybersecurity awareness, online learning confidence, responsible prompting, and practical technology skills for everyday life.

Focus Areas

  • Beginner-friendly AI skills training
  • Digital confidence for women and families
  • Cybersecurity and online safety awareness
  • Technology support for learners and teachers
  • Community-led workshops and learning resources
